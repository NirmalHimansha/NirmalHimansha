About Me

Welcome to my corner of the internet! I'm Nirmal Himansha, a web developer and technology enthusiast with a knack for creating practical, tech-driven solutions. My work often blends functionality with innovation, especially on my personal website, where I showcase my favorite projects.

Beyond web development, I'm deeply passionate about homelab setups. This hands-on approach allows me to explore cutting-edge tech while continuously learning and improving my setup.
